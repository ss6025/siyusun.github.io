TRANSLATIONAL IMPACT

Bridging Computation &
Clinical Oncology.

We partner closely with oncologists, cancer immunologists, and clinical trial teams to translate non-coding genomic discoveries into therapeutic strategies. By pairing high-resolution computational pipelines with patient biospecimens, we engineer actionable interventions and precision immunotherapy platforms.

01

Personalized Repeat Vaccines

Identifying tumor-specific cryptic peptides and neoantigens derived from transposable element activation to design custom mRNA and peptide immunotherapies.

#NeoantigenDiscovery #CancerVaccines
02

Patient Cohort Multi-Omics

Directly profiling patient biopsies via single-cell RNA-seq and spatial transcriptomics to map native tumor heterogeneity and repeat-induced immune niches.

#TranslationalCohorts #SpatialProfiling
03

Biomarker Stratification

Leveraging transposable element reactivation profiles and dsRNA sensor levels as predictive biomarkers for checkpoint inhibitor response and tumor recurrence.

#Immunotherapy #PredictiveSignatures
04

Trial-Embedded Workflows

Deploying scalable, reproducible analysis pipelines on longitudinal clinical trial cohorts to monitor dynamic immune activation and escape mechanisms.

#ClinicalTrials #TherapeuticResistance

Postdoctoral Fellow (Computational / Systems Biology)

Open

Candidates must hold or be completing a Ph.D. in Computational Biology, Bioinformatics, Computer Science, or a related field. Strong programming proficiency in Python/R, experience with high-throughput sequencing datasets (RNA-seq, single-cell multi-omics), and first-author publications are required.

Bioinformatician / Computational Staff Scientist

Open

B.S. or M.S. in Bioinformatics, Computer Science, or Data Science. Responsible for maintaining scalable data analysis workflows (Nextflow/Snakemake), managing genomic data pipelines, and contributing to primary collaborative manuscripts.
