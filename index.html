✻ OUR QUESTION

Half of the human genome is repetitive.

We ask what it remembers.

Long treated as genomic noise, repetitive DNA is a dynamic regulatory system. When its control breaks down, it can alter cell fate, activate immune pathways, and accelerate disease.

Research Program

Core Scientific Pillars

01

Transposable Elements & dsRNA

Investigating how ancient mobile genetic elements (SINEs, LINEs) become derepressed during malignant transformation, triggering double-stranded RNA (dsRNA) sensing and innate immune activation.

#LongReadSeq #RepeatGenomics #InnateImmunity
02

Epigenomics & Microenvironment

Mapping how epigenetic driver alterations reshape cellular plasticity, clonal evolution, and tumor microenvironment crosstalk via single-cell transcriptomics and spatial profiling.

#scRNAseq #SpatialGenomics #TumorEcology
03

Computational Tools & Pipelines

Building scalable, open-source software and reproducible workflows to quantify complex non-coding loci and extract signal from high-dimensional cancer genomic cohorts.

#Nextflow #Python #HighThroughputBio

Open
